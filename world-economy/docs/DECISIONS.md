## Stage 2 (Sonnet)

- **New archetype ids** (not named in Annex A, needed by Annex B POIs): `bld.showroom` (Harlan Motors, Budget Wheels, Calder Marine; `params.variant` = `used` / `marine`) and `bld.market_hall` (Harlan Wholesale Market). Everything else is a variant (`params.variant`) of an existing id: City Hall = `bld.bank` `cityhall`, 3rd Precinct = `bld.mixed_use` `police`, Metro Cab depot and BuildRight = `bld.warehouse` `taxi` / `hardware`.
- **Snapped POI coordinates** (all within the 30 m tolerance): `poi.ocean.seagrass` (-1380,1040) -> (-1410,1040), because by Annex B 4's first-match rule the annex position falls in `ocean.marina`, not `ocean.seagrass`. Other POIs keep the annex coordinate and their buildings are placed on lots beside it. `poi.lake.*` dock points for boats (`data.dock`) are in open water next to the pier, not on the shore.
- **S2 scaffold and S2 are one delivery.** The repo was empty, so the S2a scaffold (core, ids, ledger, save, harness, build, tests) and the blockout were built together. Tags: `checkpoint/stage2-blockout` on the final commit.
- **World as data, meshes as builders.** Infrastructure (bridges, towers, piers, docks, quays, breakwater, portal) is a list of world-space `parts` (boxes and cylinders) in `world/layout/infra.js`; colliders and blockout meshes are derived from the same parts. Bridge decks are `deck` colliders (sloped oriented strips) and are drawn by the road ribbon.
- **Added world API extras** (additive, signatures in section 5.5 unchanged): `World.registerInteractable/unregisterInteractable` (dynamic interactables owned by game/), `World.data` (raw builder data for renderer and tests), `World.initSteps()` (time-sliced init generator), `waterAmplitude`.
- **Road classes beyond Annex B:** `local` (Port Calder, marina and Eastside court streets), `track` (4 m access tracks generated to every POI more than 18 m beyond a road), `path` (walk-only, Annex B 5.3). Pier decks carry walk-only `path` edges so dock POIs are reachable on foot.
- **Forest road grades.** Annex B's forest terrain climbs about 12.7 %, above the 8 % / 10 % road grade limits. Paved roads keep 8 %; gravel, dirt and tracks use 11 % (path 16 %), the Fire Tower Track is routed with switchbacks (about 1.0 km instead of 0.6 km), forest hills are damped to +-9 m, and the terrain stamp cuts the remaining difference (at most about 8 m) into the hillside.
- **Far proxy** is one masked mesh pair (terrain at 25 m, LOD2 masses plus far road strips) plus a 200 m skirt out to +-6000 m with Mt. Sable; chunks that are loaded uncover their far proxy in the same frame.
- **Streaming budget under software GL:** `Streaming.pump` uses 3 ms (1.5 ms after a >15 ms frame) on real GPUs; when the renderer string contains SwiftShader/llvmpipe it uses 45 ms because frames are slow there.
- **Test scripts are .mjs/.cjs** and run with `node --test` (unit, sim) and `node test/...` (Playwright). `npm test` = unit + sim.

## Stage 3A (Opus: audio, quality system, governor, perf HUD)

No CONTRACT CHANGE: every MASTER_SPEC 5.8 / 5.11 / 5.12 signature is unchanged; the items below are additive.

- **Integration hooks (allowed S3 hook edits).** `src/main.js`: `createQuality({settings, bus})` replaces the inline `localStorage` merge; `quality.resolveAutoPreset({rendererString})` after the renderer exists (software GL now writes the *whole* Low preset, so `drawDistance` 0.7 and chunk radii [2, 5] apply under SwiftShader, previously only the resolution changed); `attachGovernor(quality)` + `governor.sample(dtReal*1000)` each frame; a minimal pixel-ratio adapter (`resize()` applies `quality.derived().pixelRatio`) for 3B to replace; F3 perf HUD; `perf.mark('ui')` / `perf.mark('audio')`; `H.setQuality` routed through Quality; `H.quality`, `H.governor`, `H.perfHud`, `H.audio`, `H.perf.allocPerFrame(frames)`; snapshot adds `quality.{preset, autoResolved, resolutionScale, governorLevel, adaptive}`, `counts.voices`, `mem.audioBufferBytes`; `Audio.attach(...)`, `Audio.setListener` + `Audio.update` each frame, `Audio.init().then(setEnvironment + music.setContext)` in the Start click, `Audio.prewarm()` at boot behind the loading screen (237 ms here). `package.json`: `npm test` also globs `test/audio/*.test.mjs`; `test:e2e` adds `test/e2e/quality.spec.cjs`; new `test:audio`; `test:all` includes it. `build.mjs` and `qa/harness.js` unchanged (harness methods live in main.js as in Stage 2).
- **Quality adapter interface (for 3B).** `quality.setAdapter({ apply(derived, effective, changedKeys) })` is called once with `['*']` and then on every user, preset or governor change. `effective` = graphics settings after governor overrides (never persisted); `derived` (pure `derive()` in `render/quality.js`) = `{ pixelRatio, resolutionScale, shadows:{enabled, cascades, mapSize, pcf, maxFar, blobShadows, casterBatchesPerCascade, lastCascadeEvery}, ao:{enabled, samples, halfRes}, bloom, godRays, postGrade, grain, reflections:{mode, planar, ssr, planarScale}, antialiasing, msaaSamples, fogCards, interiorMapping, textures:{tier, H:[albedo, normal/ORM], S, D, atlas, anisotropy}, drawDistance, chunkRadii:[near, mid], farClip, fogStartScale, lodBias, vegetation:{density, groundCoverRadius, treeCaps:[lod0, lod1]}, crowdDensity, trafficDensity, particles:{tier, maxAlive}, lightPool, uiBackdropBlur, targetFrameMs, targetFps, camera:{fov, shake, motionReduce} }`. The adapter must time-slice expensive changes (texture re-bake, shadow-map resize) behind an "Applying..." indicator. `chunkManager` still reads `settings.graphics.drawDistance` (user value); 3B should read `derived.chunkRadii` (includes governor level 11 `midRadiusDelta`). Renderers with several passes report counters with `perf.recordPass(label, calls, triangles)` and bytes with `MemTracker.addTexture / addRenderTarget`. `Quality.apply(patch)` (MASTER_SPEC 5.11) is idempotent; `subscribe(fn)` gives `{settings, effective, derived, changed, source}`; bus gets `settings:changed` and `quality:changed`.
- **Settings persistence** lives in `render/quality.js` (key `loe.settings`, storage probed with try/catch exactly like `core/save.js`), because `core/save.js` handles game slots and `data/` may not touch `localStorage`. Stored JSON is validated per field; invalid values fall back to defaults.
- **Governor** (`render/governor.js`): levels 0..11 exactly as Annex G 4.1; each step only lowers (enum `lowerOf`, numeric `min`), so a value the user set lower is never raised; resolution floor = user value - 0.3 (never below 0.5). Sliding 120-frame window, p95 > 1.08 T for 45 consecutive frames = down, mean < 0.72 T for 300 = up, 90-frame cooldown, frames > 1 s ignored (hidden tab). Allocation-free per sample.
- **Audio API additions** (additive, gameplay still plays by id only): `Audio.attach({bus, settings, getVehicle, getPlayerPos, getTime})`, `Audio.update(dt)`, `Audio.stats()`, `Audio.prewarm()`, `Audio.music.sting(id)`, `Audio.emitters.add(id, pos) / remove(key)`, `Audio.duck(bus, dB, attack, hold, release)`, `Audio.renderScene(spec, s)` and `Audio.prerenderAll()` (tests), loop handles also have `setPos(pos)`. `data/sounds.js` adds `kind:'music'|'sting'`, `seamWin`, `SOUND_BY_ID`, `REQUIRED_IDS`. `eng.generator` is an internal engine definition (used by `amb.emit.generator`), not a sound id.
- **Audio interpretations** (nearest implementation, interface kept): modal synthesis is additive (one sine per mode with its own T60) instead of a bandpass bank: same spectrum, predictable level. Two-stroke engines (`eng.outboard`, `eng.chainsaw`) fire every revolution (rpm/60 x cyl); the Annex E formula rpm/60 x cyl/2 is the four-stroke case; the test uses `firingHz()`. Engine sub-octave is 0.08 and lump AM <= 0.12, because larger values make the true waveform period 2/f and the autocorrelation fundamental stops matching the firing rate (acceptance rule); the "uneven" twin is therefore mild. Live-graph loop seams use a longer RMS window for beds with slow cycles (`seamWin`: waves 12 s, laps/frogs 8 s, gusts 4 s ...); `amb.emit.lighthouse_horn` (one blast per 30 s) is intermittent, seam N/A. `amb.forest.day` (rms 0.008-0.06, "very low level") and `amb.interior.room` (0.002-0.03, -50 dB room tone) carry their own level ranges. The <= 400 node budget is read as nodes alive at once: measured on a 2 s worst-case slice (beds + engine + music + all six one-shots) = 351; 885 nodes are created over the whole 14 s scene. Music scale membership is checked on the sequencer's note log (no polyphonic transcription); onsets are counted from the audio by spectral flux. Stingers duck the running suite's output by 8 dB rather than the whole music bus (the stinger is on that bus). Beds and music are outside the voice pool (pool = one-shots, engines, spatial loops). HRTF is given to the first 6 spatial voices alive (not re-sorted by distance each frame). Shore proximity uses optional `env.waterDist` (gameplay may supply it); until then beds follow region/district. Vehicle load is estimated from acceleration (the vehicle sim exposes normalised rpm, not throttle). Audio is statically bundled (no dynamic `import()` split yet).
- **Hitch rules for audio:** noise and texture sample data are generated at boot (`Audio.prewarm`), copied into AudioBuffers after init one per frame; bed graphs and emitters start one per update; one-shot pre-renders run one per update and are trimmed to their audible tail (171 one-shots = 20.9 MB cached, cap 24 MB).
